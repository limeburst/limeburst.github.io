Support Privacy Policy

What Kkori does

Pair your FUJIFILM X100VI over Bluetooth, allow location access, and Kkori sends GPS coordinates directly from your iPhone to your camera so your photos can be geotagged.

  • GPS sync for FUJIFILM X100VI
  • Simple Bluetooth pairing flow
  • Current location status while connected
  • Camera setup guide for reliable operation
  • Optional Always Connected unlock for background reconnect
  • Free 7-day trial for Always Connected

Your location is used only to send GPS coordinates directly to your paired camera. Kkori does not collect, store, or send your location to any server.

  • Always Connected 7 天免费试用
